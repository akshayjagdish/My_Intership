# Launch and networking plan

## Weekly routine

- Publish one small, verifiable build note or demo clip.
- Reach out to three engineers or hiring teams with a specific, respectful question about their work.
- Attend one relevant local or online engineering event and follow up with people you actually spoke with.
- Submit a small number of well-matched applications; adapt the resume to the role using evidence, not keyword stuffing.
- Track date, contact context, role, follow-up date, and next action in a private tracker.

## Outreach note template

Hi [Name] — I’m building Launchpad, a small career-planning product, and saw your work on [specific project or topic]. I’m learning about [focused question]. If you have time, I’d value a short conversation; either way, thanks for sharing your work.

Keep outreach personal and low-pressure. Do not automate unsolicited messages or claim a relationship that does not exist. Follow each platform’s rules and honor requests not to follow up.

## Application readiness checklist

- Public demo and short case study are accurate and accessible.
- Resume links to work samples and describes only completed, evidenced outcomes.
- Interview stories are practiced with clear individual contribution.
- Target roles match current skills and learning goals.
- Follow-ups are timely, concise, and respectful.
